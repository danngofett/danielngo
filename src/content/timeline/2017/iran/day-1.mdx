whilst not being able to speak or understand any Farsi at 04:55am
was certainly a challenge.

I was finally on my way to my hostel in Iranshahr, See You In Iran
Cultural House. The hostel was incredibly difficult to find since it
wasn't well known to locals.

My Taxi driver drove around asking other locals if they know of this
place, called the placed up and eventually got pointed to the right
direction which was down an alley.
