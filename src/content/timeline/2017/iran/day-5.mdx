The ride was as comfortable as the cars look... and yet there are way
worse cabs all over Iran. This was probably the scariest part about my
trip. I wouldn't do it any other way though. Where else will you get
the experience of driving at 100 doing involuntary drifting and
fishies while drinking hot af chai tea and pumping Persian club music?
