## Overview

I joined a team of 11 in 2016 and, by the end of 2019, had helped grow and lead over 25 developers across London and NYC.

As technical lead I sat in the sales-to-production pipeline, scoping custom apps and designing integrations with third-party systems. I modernised a legacy tech stack and introduced coding guidelines, code reviews and CI/CD practices across the team.

As a technical lead and former designer, I also bridged the gap between design and development through component-based design methodology, modular typography and an internal design system.

## Highlights

- Scoped and architected custom apps and third-party system integrations as part of the sales-to-production pipeline.
- Introduced coding guidelines, code review and CI/CD practices, and standardised tooling to improve quality and output.
- Facilitated technical partnerships with SaaS vendors such as Contentful, Algolia and Netlify.
- Bridged design and development with an internal design system and component-based design methodology.
- Led and grew a team of 25+ developers across London and NYC, including recruitment, interviews, training workshops and mentoring future lead developers.
- Created the agency's proprietary framework and led its move to headless development.
- Tech lead for 50+ Shopify Plus websites.
