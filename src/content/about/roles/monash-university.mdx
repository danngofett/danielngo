## Overview

In this role I helped build and maintain the university's public-facing website, [monash.edu](https://www.monash.edu/). I worked closely with the web team to implement new features and improve the user experience of the site. Later, I was involved in the development of a new design system for the university.

## Highlights

- Developed a new design system for the university, which is used across all of Monash's websites.
- Led front-end development of the [Monash Staff Portal](/projects/monash-staff-portal), an internal platform giving staff access to a range of university services and information.
- Built a new student-facing website for the university, [enrich.monash.edu](https://enrich.monash.edu/), which is used by over 100,000 students.
- Implemented new components used by the university's marketing team to build landing pages for marketing campaigns.
- Led accessibility improvements across monash.edu, building WCAG-led patterns into the shared design system.
- Improved monash.edu page load by optimising images and reducing requests.
