I’m a senior engineer who’s been building for the web since the early days. Over the past few decades, my work has ranged from graphic design, coding emails and websites for small businesses to delivering web applications on GCP under large-scale integrations across enterprise ecosystems at Monash University.

Having a background in design means I approach engineering with a product and end-user mindset. I care deeply about clean craft and best practices, page speed performance, creating joyful user experiences, and accessibility. While modern cloud architecture and API ecosystems are now central to how I deliver reliable software, I view the tools as a means to an end. I’m a developer at heart who loves experimenting with new technologies, continually learning, and shipping web software that actually solves real problems.
