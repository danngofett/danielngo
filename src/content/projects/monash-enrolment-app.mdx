The Enrolment App had been a project that was in the works for a while before I joined the team. The project was in the early stages of designing and the team was looking for a lead developer to help set up the technical foundation for the project. The project was a large-scale application that required a lot of coordination between the front-end and back-end teams, as well as many other stakeholders.

# The Challenge

Monash University is a large institution with a complex system of services and information. The Monash Enrolment App was designed to be an internal-facing platform for staff to access a range of Monash University services and information. The app was intended to be a one-stop-shop for staff and students to access all the information they need to enrol students in courses, manage student records, and access other services.
